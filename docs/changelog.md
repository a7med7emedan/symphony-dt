## 2.0.0

The first release of symphony-dt 2. It ships the SYMPHONY v2 model of record, pd_v2, frozen and sha-checked.
It adds a toolkit to build, check, calibrate, forecast with and audit Bayesian digital twins. Version 1 is preserved on the
`v1` branch and the `v1.0-preprint` tag.
